Is this a hosted subscription?

No. This release is an MIT-licensed, self-hosted application. Hosting, backups and maintenance are your responsibility. CAS-TX can discuss help with setup or customization.

What is included in v0.2?

Projects, dependency forecasts, verified progress, approved financials, resource calendars, materials, risks and approvals. Nine branded reports, scheduled email delivery, customer publications, collaboration, multiple organizations and project templates. Review the repository for the complete scope and tests.

Is it ready for my live customer data?

Validate your deployment in staging. Configure HTTPS, access, backups and attachment scanning. Optional SSO, SMTP email and Stripe hosted subscriptions use your own provider accounts. Standalone dashboard features are free; live Bidlark integration remains a separate phase.

Can CAS-TX help me run it?

Yes. Tell us about your team, hosting and workflow through the contact page. We can discuss a scoped setup or customization project.
